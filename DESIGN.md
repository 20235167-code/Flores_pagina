## Brand & Style

This design system conveys an intimate, tactile, and handcrafted sensibility tailored for bespoke crochet botanicals and woolen floral artistry. It blends artisanal warmth with modern boutique elegance.

- **Brand Personality:** Gentle, heartfelt, heirloom-quality, mindful, and restorative.
- **Target Audience:** Aesthetic gift seekers, botanical enthusiasts, sustainable craft appreciators, and home decor curators valuing lasting, handcrafted keepsakes over ephemeral cuts.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, soft nostalgia, tactile delight, and peaceful reassurance—reminiscent of unwrapping a hand-tied parcel wrapped in linen string and kraft tissue.
- **Design Movement:** Tactile Warm Minimalism. Employs soft dimensional layers, pill-form surfaces, generous breathing space, woven-like delicate outlines, and muted warm lighting rather than stark digital geometry.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from spun wool, dried botanicals, and sun-warmed linen.

- **Primary (`#D47368` / Dusty Terracotta):** Represents core floral blooms, call-to-action buttons, active navigation indicators, and primary visual anchors. Use `#E28F83` (Rose Blossom) for soft hover states, badges, and icon fills.
- **Secondary (`#7A9A7B` / Muted Sage):** Evokes hand-knit stems and foliage. Applied to secondary tags, botanical markers, organic chips, and trust signifiers.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C` / Amber Honey):** Captures floral centers, stamens, rating stars, seasonal highlights, and artisanal ribbon banners.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#2C221E` / Cozy Cocoa):** Replaces harsh jet black with rich, spun-cocoa darkness for superior reading comfort and warmth across all typographic scales.
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base surfaces utilize `#FDFBF7` (Alabaster Cream) for the viewport background, layering `#FAF6EE` (Warm Oat Linen) for cards and grouped containers, with `#F2ECE0` used for subtle structural divides.

## Typography

The type scale combines the literary, humanist warmth of **Literata** for headlines with the clean, friendly optical circles of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and body copy.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Literata`. Features organic serifs and calligraphic teardrop terminals that echo yarn loops and botanical curves. Headings should always preserve generous line heights to prevent descender crowding.
- **Body & Labels:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its friendly open counters and rounded apexes offer effortless legibility on high-density mobile screens while retaining an approachable, hand-set look.
- **Editorial Elements:** Use italicized `Literata` for quotes, maker notes, and botanical Latin naming (e.g., *Rosa gallica tejida*).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes mobile-first storytelling, transitioning smoothly into a curated editorial spread on wider viewports.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (<640px): 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
  - Tablet (640px–1024px): 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - Desktop (>1024px): 12-column layout centered at max width `1200px` with `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an organic `0.5rem` base unit. Section separation favors ample vertical space (`space-xl` and higher) to evoke breathable, boutique catalog styling. Content containers remain grounded and cozy with balanced internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient lighting paired with soft tactile borders rather than cold synthetic drop shadows.

- **Surface Tonal Layers:**
  - Base canvas: `#FDFBF7`.
  - Raised card / container surface: `#FAF6EE`.
  - Floating element / sheet overlay: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Soft Ambient Tinting:** Shadows use a warm cocoa dispersion tint `rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.06)` with wide diffusion radii:
  - Low elevation (Cards, controls): `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.03)`.
  - Medium elevation (Bottom sheets, popovers): `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.04)`.
  - High elevation (Hero floating badges): `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.1)`.
- **Tactile Outlines:** Cards and interactive fields combine subtle drop shadows with a fine, warm border: `1px solid rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.07)` or `#EFE8DB`. This creates a crisp, crafted card boundary reminiscent of handmade stationery.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a fluid, pill-centric language reflecting the rounded nature of yarn loops, crochet rings, and soft botanical petals.

- **Base Corner Radius (`1rem`):** Applied to standard content cards, image wrappers, and nested layout blocks.
- **Large Corner Radius (`rounded-lg: 2rem`):** Applied to feature story panels, bottom drawer headers, and hero product showcases.
- **Extra Large Corner Radius (`rounded-xl: 3rem`):** Used for fluid section separators, organic callout cards, and dynamic background containers.
- **Pill Form (Full `9999px`):** Reserved for interactive buttons, badge indicators, search bars, filter tags, and quantity selectors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill-shaped geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`). Background `#D47368`, text `#FFFFFF`, with `1px solid #C46257`. Active states press inward with `transform: scale(0.98)` and shift to `#C46257`.
- **Secondary:** Pill-shaped outline button with background `#FAF6EE`, text `#2C221E`, and border `1.5px solid #7A9A7B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or background; text `#D47368` or `#2C221E` with subtle underline on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Petite height (`24px–28px`), `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. 
- **Palette Variants:** 
  - Sage Craft chip: Background `rgba(122, 154, 123, 0.15)`, text `#3D593E`.
  - Blossom tag: Background `rgba(212, 115, 104, 0.15)`, text `#8E372E`.
  - Amber Honey award: Background `rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.18)`, text `#6E4E10`.

### Product & Story Cards
- Background `#FAF6EE`, border `1px solid #EAE2D3`, rounded to `1.25rem`.
- Imagery uses soft inset padding (`0.5rem`) with an inner rounded image frame (`0.875rem`) to mimic framed botanical artwork.
- Subtle warm lift on press or hover with an ambient cocoa glow.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature generous padding (`0.75rem 1.25rem`), pill or high-radius borders (`1.5rem`), background `#FDFBF7`, and border `1px solid #DFD6C7`.
- Focus state activates an outline in `#7A9A7B` with a gentle 3px diffuse ring: `rgba(122, 154, 123, 0.25)`.
- Placeholder text is rendered in `rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.45)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls are fully circular; checkboxes have soft `0.375rem` corners.
- Selected state fills with `#7A9A7B` or `#D47368` containing an off-white embroidered-style check icon. Unchecked border is `1.5px solid #C5BBAE`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Yarn Care Instruction Tiles:** Horizontal mini-cards featuring illustrated wash/care symbols in `#7A9A7B` beside soft descriptions.
- **Bouquet Customizer Swatches:** Circular yarn color selectors (`36px` diameter) with an active nested double ring to display chosen petal and stem yarn shades.
- **Artisan Signature Tag:** A parchment-like footer or card segment signed in `Literata` italic with an artisan stamp graphic in `#E28F83`.
